Services

What we do.

Everything we offer comes down to one promise: the 4D system your business depends on will keep working, keep improving, and never again depend on a single person — including any one of us.

Consulting & development

New systems · Modernization · Integration

We build new 4D applications and we modernize existing ones. Most of our work is the second kind: a system that has run a business for ten or twenty years and now needs to meet the present — a current 4D version, project mode, ORDA and classes, a web or mobile front end, or a connection to something outside itself.

  • New systems. Designed with you, from first conversation through deployment. 4D lets us build quickly for a small operation and keep building as it grows, without switching tools.
  • Modernization. Binary to project mode. Legacy code to ORDA and object-oriented 4D. Compiled, version-controlled, documented, and running on a supported release — on Mac, Windows, or both.
  • Integration. Accounting platforms (QuickBooks Online, Xero), CAD and CNC systems, e-commerce and marketplace hosts, warehouse and shipping operations, payment and POS systems, web services and APIs.
  • Web and mobile. 4D's web server and REST layer, with modern front ends where your users need them — without losing the density and keyboard-first design that line-of-business users depend on.

Inheriting a 4D system

Assessment · Recovery · A path forward

This is the call we get most often: the developer who built your system has retired, moved on, or died, and you are responsible for software nobody on your staff understands. Sometimes you have the source code. Sometimes you don't. Occasionally the application carries a hard-coded expiration date nobody told you about.

We have done this many times. The first step is always an assessment — what you have, what state it's in, what it would take to make it safe — and an honest conversation about your options before anyone commits to anything.

  • Assessment. Version, platform, source availability, data integrity, licensing, and the real risks. A written report you can act on with or without us.
  • Recovery. Data recovery from orphaned datafiles. Reconstruction when source is missing. Stabilizing a system that is failing today.
  • Stabilize, then modernize. Get it into version control, documented, and on a supported release first. Then decide, together, what it should become.

Professional training

Teams · Individuals · Modern 4D

The best insurance against dependence on any single developer — us included — is capable people inside your own organization. We train developers and teams at every level, on your codebase or ours.

  • Onboarding. Bringing a new developer onto an existing 4D application: how it's built, how it's deployed, where the bodies are buried.
  • Modern 4D. ORDA, classes, the web server, project mode, version control, and current best practices for developers who learned 4D a long time ago.
  • AI-assisted development for 4D. Setting up and working effectively with AI coding tools on 4D projects — context files, brownfield refactoring, and knowing what to trust.

Long-term stewardship

Maintenance · Support · Succession

Ongoing maintenance and support plans for the systems we build and the systems we inherit. More than one of us knows every system we steward, and every plan is built so that you could walk away from us at any time and lose nothing.

  • You own your code. Source lives in a repository you control. We work in it; we don't hold it hostage.
  • You own your data. Documented schema, exportable data, no proprietary lock-in beyond the platform itself.
  • You own the knowledge. Documentation written as we go, not promised at the end. Tests where they matter. A system anyone competent could pick up.
  • Succession is planned. Not an afterthought — the reason the firm exists.
