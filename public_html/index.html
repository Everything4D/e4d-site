Why E4D

Built for the long run.

Much of the world's 4D development has historically been done by lone-wolf developers — a single trusted expert who knows the system inside and out. It works beautifully, right up until it doesn't. When that developer retires, or can't continue, the business is left with a critical system and no one who understands it. Some of our clients came to us in exactly that position.

We formed Everything 4D specifically to solve that problem — a partnership deliberately spanning generations, so the firm outlives any one developer.

Our three founders bring complementary careers to the table: decades of 4D development reaching back to the late 1980s, a software business built and run in England, and experience inside one of the largest 4D installations in the world — a multibillion-dollar company that runs its entire operation on the platform. The same platform scales down gracefully: we use 4D to build quickly for small operations, without having to switch tools when a project grows.

We are a new company by incorporation date. We are not new at this.
