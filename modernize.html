Refresh and revive your system

Current 4D · Modern code · Fewer headaches

A system that has run your business for ten or twenty years doesn't need throwing away — it needs bringing up to date. We move it onto a current, supported 4D release and tidy up what has built up over the years, so it's ready for the next decade rather than just the next crisis.

  • Up to date. A supported 4D version on current Mac and Windows, in project mode and under version control.
  • Easier to live with. The niggles your staff have worked around for years, finally fixed.
  • Documented. So it never again depends on one person's memory.

Web-enable your business applications

Browser · Mobile · Connected

Your people shouldn't have to be at a particular desk to use the system. We put the parts that matter on the web — for staff on the road, customers checking an order, or suppliers updating stock — while the core application carries on doing what it does best.

  • Anywhere access. Browser and mobile screens built on 4D's own web server and REST layer.
  • Connected. Links to the services you already use — accounting, e-commerce, shipping, payments.
  • No compromises. The fast, keyboard-first screens your power users rely on stay exactly where they are.

Replace your system and bring your data back to life

Migration · Recovery · Reporting

Sometimes the right answer is something new. Years of valuable information may be locked inside an ageing database, a tangle of spreadsheets, or an application nobody can maintain any more. We build a replacement that fits how you work today, and carry your history across with it — cleaned up, connected, and ready to use.

  • Nothing left behind. Your existing data migrated, checked and reconciled.
  • Recovered. Information rescued even from orphaned or damaged datafiles.
  • Useful again. Searchable, reportable and joined up, instead of sitting in an archive.

Make your system work for you

Automation · Insight · Less re-keying

Too many businesses end up working around their software. We turn that round: the system should do the repetitive work, tell you what's going on, and fit the way your team actually operates.

  • Automated. Routine jobs, documents and emails that run themselves.
  • Informed. Reports and dashboards that answer the questions you actually ask.
  • Joined up. Enter it once, use it everywhere — no more copying between systems.

Phased · Clear scope · Yours to keep
